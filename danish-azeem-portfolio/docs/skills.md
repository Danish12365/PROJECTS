# Skills

Grouped by how they show up in day-to-day analytics and modeling work.

---

## Languages & core analytics

!!! tip "Python ecosystem"
    **Python**, **Pandas**, and **NumPy** for data cleaning, feature work, reproducible pipelines, and notebook-ready storytelling.

---

## Machine learning & deep learning

!!! success "Modeling stack"
    **Scikit-Learn** for strong baselines and classical ML; **TensorFlow** when the problem benefits from deep learning; ensemble and hybrid ideas where lift justifies complexity.

---

## Visualization & BI

!!! note "From notebook to boardroom"
    **Matplotlib** and **Seaborn** for analysis visuals; **Power BI** with **DAX** for interactive dashboards and business-friendly reporting.

---

## Data platforms & productivity

!!! abstract "Data access & spreadsheets"
    **MySQL** for querying structured sources; **Excel** for quick validation, stakeholder handoffs, and lightweight modeling.

---

## Modern AI tooling

!!! warning "LLM-assisted workflows"
    **LLM tools** for drafting, code assistance, documentation, and accelerated exploration—always grounded in checks against real data and domain constraints.
